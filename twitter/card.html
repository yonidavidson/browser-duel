Three experiments: small typed AI decisions vs full LLMs

Same tasks, same data, measured — where does a fast decision model win, and where does it not?

🔤 subtext

pub/sub where subscriptions are written in English
one TypeSafe request per message · 13 judgments
semantic routing beats the lexical mock on every hard case
9 messages · $0.0004 · ~440 ms/message
deliver / review / drop — thresholds you can drag.

📡 radio-vision

name the modulation from 128 raw IQ samples
11 modulations · AWGN at any SNR (−20…+30 dB)
14-feature representation → 7-NN classifier
receiver: 70% modulation · 96% group at +30 dB
confusions land exactly on the thesis families (8PSK↔QPSK, QAM16↔64, CPFSK↔GFSK).

⚔️ browser-duel

Jev vs GPT-5.6: who picks better links in a wiki race?
Jev $0.00012 · ~0.4 s per decision
gpt-5.6-sol $0.0035 · ~4.7 s per decision
easy task: all tie (2 clicks) · hard task: GPT 5–8 clicks, Jev gives up
hybrid (Jev, escalating when unsure) — the confidence gate is the next story.
